      Review notes

      How to interpret these results & display settings

      A shape screen, not a tracking diagnosis. The existing rule suggests review when at least 8 candidates are measured and at least 40% have a major-to-minor axis ratio of 1.7 or more. No thresholds were changed for this report.

      Limits. Galaxies and other extended objects can be mistaken for star candidates: the analyzer measures shape without identifying the object. Close pairs, interpolation, optics, noise and motion can also affect shape. Large or saturated regions may be excluded. A frame without an elongation flag can still have defects. The brightest-quarter statistic is a descriptive cross-check, not a confidence score.

      Coverage. The overview shows up to 120 candidates sampled across the contrast ranking. The crop gallery samples both shape groups. The CSVs retain the complete measurements.

      Display only. Enhanced view maps 16-bit luminance into a visible range using a per-exposure 1st–99.98th percentile range and an asinh curve. It also reveals noise and may clip bright preview values. Linear view uses the full original numeric range. Neither changes the source images, CSV measurements or decisions.

      Fair comparisons. All crops within an exposure share its stretch. Different exposures have separately estimated stretches, so preview brightness is not a photometric comparison. The overview is downsampled; candidate crops use native pixels.
